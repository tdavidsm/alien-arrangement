How to use the table

  • Move a card: drag it. It snaps to the grid when you let go.
  • Move the table: drag an empty spot to pan around.
  • Zoom: pinch with two fingers, or use + / − .
  • Look closely: double-tap a card to zoom right in on it.
  • See it all: tap Fit to frame your whole arrangement.
  • Stacks: drop a card on another to stack them. Drag the top card off to unstack. A badge shows how many are in a stack.
